# Memoir 1.2.1

- Fixes a meeting window that could open taller than the screen and refuse to shrink.
- Repairs oversized saved window frames when opening or reopening the library.
- Uses a smaller default window and permits shorter window heights.
- Recording controls scroll when space is limited; the meeting list and transcript continue scrolling independently.

Compatible with Memoir Processor 1.2.0. Requires macOS 15 or later.
